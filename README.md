# 📊 Electronic Sales – Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

**Electronic Sales – Sales & Product Insights** is an interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze the sales performance of an electronic retail business.

The dashboard provides insights into **sales, profit, orders, quantity sold, product performance, category performance, city-wise sales, payment methods, and monthly/yearly sales trends**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Track monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Analyze sales performance across different cities.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Compare sales and quantity across categories.
- Identify top-selling products.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive dashboards.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses an electronic sales dataset containing approximately **8,000 sales transactions** from **2023 to 2025**.

### Dataset Details

- **Rows:** 8,000
- **Years:** 2023, 2024, 2025
- **Product Categories:** Smartphones, Laptops, Televisions, Refrigerators, Washing Machines, Air Conditioners, Tablets, Smartwatches, Headphones, Accessories
- **Payment Methods:** EMI, Credit Card, UPI, Debit Card, Cash
- **Order Types:** Walk-in, Online
- **Delivery Types:** Store Pickup, Home Delivery

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The business generated approximately **₹283M in total sales** and **₹57M in total profit**.
- Around **8K orders** generated more than **10K units sold**.
- **Smartphones** were the highest-performing category, followed by **Laptops**.
- **October** recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately **₹32.8M**.
- **February** recorded the lowest monthly sales at approximately **₹18.6M**.
- **iPhone 15** was identified as the top-selling product.
- **EMI and Credit Card** were major payment methods contributing to sales.
- The dashboard helps compare performance across products, categories, cities, and time periods.
